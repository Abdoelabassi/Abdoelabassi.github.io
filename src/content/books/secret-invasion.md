The payoff to years of Bendis's paranoia-fuelled New Avengers. A Skrull ship crashes in the Savage Land, the Skrulls attack in force, and the Marvel Universe has to work out who is real. Leinil Francis Yu draws widescreen chaos, and the ending leads straight into Norman Osborn's Dark Reign.
