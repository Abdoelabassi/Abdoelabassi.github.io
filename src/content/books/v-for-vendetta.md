Moore and Lloyd's dystopia began in Warrior magazine in 1982 and was completed for DC in 1988. Lloyd's shadow-heavy art, with no thought balloons or sound effects, makes Norsefire's London feel cold and claustrophobic. The book still asks the uncomfortable question: where does the freedom fighter end and the terrorist begin?
