The event that rebuilt Marvel's cosmic corner. It's a war story on a galactic scale: Nova, Drax, the Silver Surfer, Ronan and Super-Skrull, with Thanos and Galactus caught in the middle. Its aftermath led directly to Abnett and Lanning's Guardians of the Galaxy.
